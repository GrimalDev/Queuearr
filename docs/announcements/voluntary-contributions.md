# Queuearr is evolving: optional contributions

We're preparing an optional $5 monthly contribution through Queuearr to help cover the infrastructure and operating costs of all the services we provide. We'll lower the price in the future if costs allow.

When payment prompts are enabled, you can choose Pay now to open the payment link or scan its QR code, or Pay later to keep using the services. After making a payment, select I've paid to pause reminders for one month. Choosing Pay later brings the reminder back on your next visit.

Contributions are voluntary and based on trust. There are no automatic charges, and choosing not to pay does not block access.
